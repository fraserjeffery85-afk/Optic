👋 **New here? Meet Optic.** Optic is a free Windows app that makes the dark parts of your games easier to see, brightening shadows and boosting colour while you ride, then putting your screen straight back to normal when you leave the game. It's made for MX Bikes riders (and works with other games too), on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics. I built it because ReShade looks great but can crash MX Bikes, so Optic changes your screen's colours from outside the game instead: it never touches the game files, so it can't crash it.
